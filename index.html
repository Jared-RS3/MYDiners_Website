FAQs

Everything you’re wondering, answered before your first bite.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but weekend nights fill up fast. Booking ahead keeps your table.

Can you take a big group?

Yes. Tell us how many are coming and we’ll put the tables together.

Can I get it as takeaway?

Everything on the menu travels. Order at the counter or call ahead.

Is there anything for vegetarians?

Plenty: paneer makhani, dhal tadka, veg biryani and all the sides.

Do you do birthdays?

Birthdays are our favourite. Mention it when you book and leave the rest to us.

Will I stay longer than planned?

Almost certainly. Nobody leaves after just one naan.
